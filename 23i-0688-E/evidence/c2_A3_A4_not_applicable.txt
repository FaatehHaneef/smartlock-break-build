C.2 A3 (keying) and A4 (nonce) re-run against v2 - NOT APPLICABLE

A3: v2 uses 2048-bit ephemeral DH, signed. The DH message carries no small
    (p,g) group and B is 256 bytes, so baby-step/giant-step is infeasible.
    length of captured B (hex chars): 512
    DH message has a signature field (sig:) : yes
    And keys come from HKDF (not the raw secret).

A4: each record carries an 8-byte sequence number -> a unique nonce per
    record; the two directions use independent HKDF keys. No keystream reuse.
    Per-record seqno headers (first 16 hex of each rec):
           1	0000000000000000
           2	0000000000000000
           3	0000000000000001
           4	0000000000000001
           5	0000000000000002
           6	0000000000000002
           7	0000000000000003
           8	0000000000000003
